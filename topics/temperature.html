Temperature

Temperature is a setting that controls how random or creative an LLM's responses are — low temperature means predictable and focused, high temperature means varied and creative.

What is Temperature?

When an LLM generates text, it predicts the probability of each possible next token. Temperature controls how those probabilities are used:

  • Temperature 0: Always picks the most likely token. Deterministic, consistent, but potentially boring or repetitive.
  • Temperature 0.5-0.7: Mostly picks likely tokens but allows some variation. Good balance for most tasks.
  • Temperature 1.0+: More willing to pick less likely tokens. More creative and surprising, but may be less coherent.

When to Adjust Temperature

Low temperature (0-0.3): Use for factual questions, code generation, data extraction, math — tasks where there's one correct answer.
Medium temperature (0.5-0.7): Use for general conversation, writing, summarization.
High temperature (0.8-1.0): Use for brainstorming, creative writing, poetry — tasks where variety is valued.

Layman's Analogy

Temperature is like a "creativity dial." At zero, the AI is like a strict accountant — always giving the safest, most expected answer. Turn it up, and it becomes more like a jazz musician — improvising, taking risks, sometimes brilliant, sometimes off-key. You adjust based on whether you need precision or inspiration.
