Transformer

The Transformer is a neural network architecture introduced in 2017 that revolutionized AI by enabling models to process entire sequences of data in parallel using a mechanism called "attention."

What is a Transformer?

The Transformer is the architecture behind all modern large language models — including Claude, GPT, Gemini, and Llama. It was introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need" by researchers at Google. Before Transformers, AI models processed text one word at a time (sequentially). Transformers process all words simultaneously, making them much faster to train and much better at understanding relationships between distant parts of a text.

The Key Innovation: Attention

The "attention mechanism" lets the model look at all parts of the input at once and decide which parts are most relevant to each other. In the sentence "The cat sat on the mat because it was tired," attention helps the model understand that "it" refers to "cat" — even though other words sit between them.

Why It Matters

Transformers enabled the scaling revolution in AI. Because they process data in parallel, they can be trained on massive datasets using thousands of GPUs simultaneously. This scalability is what made billion-parameter models like Claude possible. Every major AI breakthrough since 2017 has been built on the Transformer architecture.

Layman's Analogy

Imagine reading a mystery novel. Older AI was like reading one word at a time, forgetting earlier pages. A Transformer is like being able to see every page simultaneously — spotting that a clue on page 3 connects to the revelation on page 200. The "attention" mechanism is your finger connecting those two passages with a line.
