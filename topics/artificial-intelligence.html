Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence is the field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence — such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, making decisions, and learning from experience.

What is AI?

AI is a broad term covering any computer system designed to mimic human cognitive abilities. This ranges from simple rule-based systems (like spam filters) to complex neural networks that can write essays, generate images, or drive cars.

Modern AI is dominated by machine learning — systems that learn from data rather than following hand-written rules. The most powerful current AI systems are large language models like Claude, which can understand and generate human language.

Types of AI

Narrow AI (ANI): AI designed for one specific task — like playing chess, translating languages, or recommending movies. All current AI is narrow AI.

General AI (AGI): A hypothetical AI that could perform any intellectual task a human can. This does not yet exist.

Superintelligent AI (ASI): A hypothetical AI that surpasses human intelligence in every domain. This is a subject of safety research at companies like Anthropic.

Key Subfields

  • Machine Learning — Learning from data
  • Deep Learning — Neural networks with many layers
  • Natural Language Processing — Understanding human language
  • Computer Vision — Understanding images and video
  • Robotics — AI controlling physical machines

Layman's Analogy

AI is like teaching a computer to think. Just as a child learns to recognize cats by seeing thousands of pictures of cats, an AI system learns to perform tasks by processing large amounts of examples. The difference is that computers can process millions of examples in hours, while humans take years.
