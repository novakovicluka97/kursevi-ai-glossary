Claude Design

Claude Design is a collaborative visual creation tool by Anthropic Labs that lets users generate designs, prototypes, slides, and marketing materials through natural-language prompts.

What is Claude Design?

Launched on April 17, 2026, Claude Design is an AI-powered design tool that turns text descriptions into visual assets. You describe what you want — a landing page mockup, a presentation slide, a one-pager, a marketing poster — and Claude Design creates it. You can then iterate by giving feedback in natural language or editing elements directly.

Key Features

  • Prompt-to-design: Describe what you want, get a visual output
  • Inline editing: Comment on specific elements, edit text directly, tweak spacing/color/layout
  • Design system support: Apply your company's brand guidelines automatically
  • Export options: PDF, PPTX, Canva, standalone HTML
  • Handoff to code: Package designs into a bundle that Claude Code can implement

Who is it for?

Claude Design targets non-designers who need visual output — marketers creating campaign materials, product managers making mockups, founders building pitch decks, or teams creating internal presentations. It doesn't replace professional design tools like Figma for complex work, but handles the "80% of design tasks that don't need a designer."

Layman's Analogy

Imagine describing a poster to an artist over the phone: "Make it blue, put the logo top-left, big headline saying 'Summer Sale', product photo in the center." Claude Design is that artist — except instant, tireless, and able to produce dozens of variations in seconds.
